# What is the Numogram?

According to the CCRU (Cybernetic Culture Research Unit), the Numogram — also called the **Decimal Labyrinth** — is a *time-map*: a diagram built entirely out of the digits 0–9 and the arithmetical relations between them. It is one half of **Pandemonium**, the CCRU's "complete system of Lemurian demonism and time sorcery," the other half being the *Matrix*, which lists the names, numbers and attributes of the demons associated with each connection.

The whole system is deliberately mechanical. It "is constructed according to immanent criteria latent in decimal numeracy, and involves only basic arithmetical operations (assembled from additions and subtractions)." In other words: no symbolism is imported — the structure falls out of base-10 arithmetic itself.

## Zones

The diagram is composed of **ten zones**, numbered 0–9. A zone is not a number in the mathematical sense; in CCRU usage it is a *place* — somewhere a "sorcerous" traveller enters, dwells in, and leaves. On the diagram, each zone is drawn as a circle containing its digit.

## Syzygies

The ten zones are grouped into **five pairs (syzygies)** by *nine-sum twinning* — the CCRU term is *zygonovism*. Every zone pairs with the digit that completes it to 9:

| Syzygy | Name    |
|--------|---------|
| 9::0   | Uttunul |
| 8::1   | Murrumur|
| 7::2   | Oddubb  |
| 6::3   | Djynxx  |
| 5::4   | Katak   |

The five twin-pairs are themselves entities — the syzygetic demons or "lemurs" of the system.

## Currents

The arithmetical **difference** within each syzygy defines a **current** — a flow toward a "tractor zone." The five differences (9, 7, 5, 3, 1) yield all the odd digits. Currents are the *primary flows* of the numogram — the large circulating arrows dominating the diagram.

The arrangement of the currents divides the Maze into three time-systems:

- **Time-Circuit** — the currents of the three central syzygies compose a cycle rotating in anticlockwise steps; the inner loop of "ordinary" time (zones 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8).
- **Warp** — the upper syzygy's current folds back into (a half of) itself, an autonomous loop (zones 3, 6).
- **Plex** — the lower syzygy's current does the same (zones 0, 9).

Warp and Plex are "of an intrinsically cryptic nature," variously considered *Outside-* or *Outer-time*.

## Gates and channels

Each zone number, when **digitally cumulated** (summed 1→n, i.e. its triangular number), defines the value of a **gate**; the *digital reduction* of that value sets the course of a **channel**. Channels are the *secondary flows* — "time-holes, or secret interconnections" — the thinner lines crossing the diagram.

Example: zone 7 → cumulation 28 → reduction 2+8=10 → 1+0=**1**, so gate 28 opens a channel to zone 1.

| Zone | Gate | Channel to |
|------|------|------------|
| 0    | 00*  | 0          |
| 1    | 01   | 1          |
| 2    | 03   | 3          |
| 3    | 06   | 6          |
| 4    | 10   | 1          |
| 5    | 15   | 6          |
| 6    | 21   | 3          |
| 7    | 28   | 1          |
| 8    | 36   | 9          |
| 9    | 45   | 9          |

\* Gate 0 is "an explicitly disputed case" in the source tradition.

The gates and channels "knit the Maze together, providing connections between otherwise incompatible time-systems. They open and close the ways of sorcerous traffic. Although each gate deranges time in its own way, their operations vary with a certain regional consistency."

## The demons

Each of the 45 pairs of distinct zones is a **gate**, and each gate is inhabited by a demon of the Pandemonium Matrix (e.g. Katak, Mur Mur, Oddubb, Takix). The demons are classified by how they relate to the time-systems — e.g. **chronodemons** (varieties of distance *within* the Time-Circuit) and **amphidemons** (ruptures in the circuit, "openings to the Outside").

## Reading the SVG in this repo

`Numogram.svg` is the standard published rendering of the decimal numogram:

- The **circles with digits** are the ten zones.
- The **small rings** beside them are the syzygy pairings.
- The **large curved arrows** (the gradient-faded paths) are the currents — notice how the three central syzygies' currents form the rotating Time-Circuit while the top and bottom pairs fold back into the Warp and Plex loops.
- The **thin lines and arrowheads** threading the map are the gates' channels.
