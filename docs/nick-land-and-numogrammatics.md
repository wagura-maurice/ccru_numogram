# Nick Land and Numogrammatics

## Where it comes from

The Numogram is the central artifact of the **CCRU** — the Cybernetic Culture Research Unit, the experimental theory collective Nick Land was associated with at the University of Warwick in the mid-to-late 1990s. In CCRU lore the diagram was *received* rather than invented: an artifact "unearthed" from a partially occulted "Lemurian" tradition of time-sorcery, transmitted through pulp-occult lineages (the "Cthulhu Club," the fictitious Nma, Professor Challenger). Whether taken as myth or method, the framing matters: Land treats the numogram as a found *machine*, not a symbol someone designed.

It is one component of **Pandemonium** — the numogram is the map, the Matrix is the demonology layered over it.

## What it is *for*

### A time-map / time-machine

Land consistently describes the numogram as "a time map, a time maze." Its regions — the rotating **Time-Circuit**, and the self-folding **Warp** and **Plex** — model different regimes of time: ordinary cyclic time in the middle, "Outside-time" at the poles. The currents and channels are routes between them. In the CCRU's vocabulary, using the map is *time-sorcery*: procedures for moving between, and "deranging," incompatible time-systems. The much-hyped description of it as a *time-travelling device* is kept deliberately ambiguous — it functions as a diagram for thinking about time as something traversable and perturbable rather than a linear sequence.

### A hyperstitional engine

The numogram is a canonical example of **hyperstition** — Land's term for fictions that make themselves real. It is a piece of invented occult machinery that produces real effects: a whole demonology (the Pandemonium Matrix), a literature, a subculture, and a research programme have been generated simply by *working* the diagram. The CCRU wiki calls it "an abstract machine endowed with hyperstructural complexity" — a machine for generating hyperstitional interactions.

### Decimal occultism — "numogrammatics"

Land's practice with it is **numogrammatics**: a rigorously arithmetical occultism. Unlike hermeneutic qabbalah, which reads meaning *into* symbols, numogrammatics derives everything procedurally from decimal operations — nine-sum twinning, digital cumulation, digital reduction. The point is that structure *emerges* from base-10 itself: the five syzygies, the odd-number currents, the gate table. This makes it a kind of experimental instrument — you run operations on it and observe what the system produces.

### A stripped-down Tree of Life

Land has drawn the comparison explicitly: like the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, the numogram is a map of ten zones and the paths between them — but rebuilt on purely decimal, immanent arithmetic, with no imported theology. It is occult diagram-craft reduced to machinery.

## Its place in Land's thought

The numogram connects directly to the themes that define Land's work:

- **Time as material** — consistent with his accelerationism: time is not a neutral container but a system with regions, currents, and fault lines that can be navigated or shorted.
- **The Outside** — the Warp and Plex embody exteriority breaking into the circuit, the same "Outside" that runs through *Fanged Noumena* and his Lovecraft reading.
- **Immanence** — the diagram needs no external meaning; its demonology precipitates out of arithmetic, mirroring his preference for systems that run themselves.

The numogram survives the CCRU era: it recurs throughout Land's later writing (e.g. on the *Hyperstition* blog and in interviews), where it functions less as an object of belief than as a working model — a compact engine for thinking about number, time, and self-realizing fiction.
